隐私政策

应用名称：证件照（IDPhotoStudio）　最后更新：2026-07-04

本应用非常重视你的隐私。我们的原则很简单：不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何信息。

我们如何处理你的照片

你选择的照片仅在你的设备本地进行处理（抠图、换底色、排版）。所有处理都在设备上完成，不会联网、不会上传到任何服务器。生成的证件照是否保存到相册，完全由你决定。

我们收集的信息

无。本应用不注册账号、不请求网络、不使用任何第三方分析或广告 SDK，不收集设备标识、位置或任何个人数据。

权限说明

本应用仅在你主动操作时访问：相册（读取你选择的照片、以及在你点击保存时写入生成的证件照）。除此之外不访问其它数据。

Privacy Policy

App: IDPhotoStudio　Last updated: 2026-07-04

This app takes your privacy seriously. Our principle is simple: we do not collect, upload, or share any of your information.

How we handle your photos

Photos you select are processed entirely on your device (cutout, background replacement, layout). Nothing is sent over the network or uploaded to any server. Whether a generated photo is saved to your library is entirely your choice.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, makes no network requests, and includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect device identifiers, location, or any personal data.

Permissions

The app accesses your photo library only when you act: to read the photo you pick, and to write the generated photo when you tap save. Nothing else is accessed.
